How lighting works on this pad

The app exposes only the firmware's six built-in effects and eight palette colours. All animated firmware modes — including Reactive — can fire on both key-down and key-up, and that can't be changed from the host. Solid, White and Off are the only release-free effects. There is no per-key RGB and no custom-RGB mode in this firmware; the newer SIKAI RGB-table packet was tested on this unit and ignored.
